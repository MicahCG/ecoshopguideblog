# Bambana Christmas Aesthetics Art Direction

Prepared September 9, 2026, America/Chicago

Status: complete. Six destination-specific creative assets were generated from verified product references, three Shopify landing pages were published and checked on desktop plus a 430 x 932 mobile viewport, and three Amazon Idea Lists were verified publicly. No Make routes were activated or run.

## Shared campaign system

- Format: 1024 x 1536, exact 2:3 Pinterest portrait.
- Deliverables: three aesthetic masters, copied into one Shopify and one Amazon route file per aesthetic, six route files total.
- Matching rule: use the same composition across destinations when both assortments support it. If an identifiable product differs, generate a tightly matched destination-specific variant instead of implying the pictured product is available at both destinations.
- Hierarchy: one clear hero styling moment, two to four identifiable gift or decor anchors, a believable room and a calm path for the eye.
- Brand: cream, forest green, natural texture, warm editorial lighting and grounded copy.
- Avoid: product grids disguised as lifestyle scenes, piles of wrapped boxes, unreadable overlays, unsupported counts, fake labels, logos and watermarks.
- Copy: optional concise overlay only when it improves purchase intent. Destination title and description should do most of the selling.

## 1. White & Gold Clean Christmas

Proposed Shopify handle: `white-gold-christmas`

Landing-page title: `White & Gold Christmas`

Hero heading: `A lighter, calmer way to celebrate.`

Hero body: `Build a polished holiday room with soft ivory, warm gold and a few well-chosen details that let the space breathe.`

Creative direction:

- White or softly flocked tree with sparse warm lights.
- Ivory linen, cream boucle, pale wood and brushed or aged gold.
- Clean gift wrapping with tonal ribbon and generous negative space.
- Architectural room with daylight plus warm lamp light, never sterile white-on-white.
- Verified Shopify anchors: tall gold tree, cream knit stocking and clear glass ornaments. Verified Amazon anchors: ivory knit stockings, gold stocking holders and a white ceramic village. A full-size white tree is atmospheric only.

Optional overlay: `A calm Christmas, beautifully considered`

Pin title: `White and gold Christmas decor for a calm, polished home`

Prompt scaffold:

> Photorealistic editorial Christmas living room in ivory and warm white, a restrained white tree, brushed-gold ornaments, tonal gifts and pale natural wood. Vertical 2:3. Clean negative space, warm daylight, believable materials. Feature the destination-specific verified product anchors faithfully. No people, brands, logos, unsupported text or clutter.

## 2. Boho Christmas

Proposed Shopify handle: `boho-christmas`

Landing-page title: `Boho Christmas`

Hero heading: `Warm texture, natural color, easy holiday comfort.`

Hero body: `Layer wood, woven pieces, soft textiles and relaxed greenery for a Christmas room that feels collected instead of staged.`

Creative direction:

- Natural evergreen tree with warm lights and handmade-feeling ornaments.
- Walnut, rattan, linen, wool, terracotta and muted brass.
- Cozy seating and one gift-in-use moment, such as bedding, pillow or sleep-care set.
- Asymmetrical styling and lived-in warmth without visual mess.
- Verified Shopify anchors: Folk Copper plaid throw, natural scalloped stripe napkins and wooden tree lights. Verified Amazon anchors: rustic wooden ornaments, orange cheesecloth runner and rust fleece throw.

Optional overlay: `A warmer, more natural Christmas`

Pin title: `Boho Christmas ideas for a warm, collected home`

Prompt scaffold:

> Photorealistic editorial boho Christmas interior with warm wood, woven texture, natural greenery, relaxed ornaments, linen and a cozy amber glow. Vertical 2:3. Feature the destination-specific verified products in believable use. Layered but calm, lived-in and shoppable. No people, brands, logos, fake labels, unsupported text or product piles.

## 3. Maximalist Christmas

Proposed Shopify handle: `maximalist-christmas`

Landing-page title: `Maximalist Christmas`

Hero heading: `More color. More pattern. More joy.`

Hero body: `Layer classic red, deep green and warm gold while keeping every piece intentional and easy to shop.`

Creative direction:

- Full green tree or greenery with classic red and warm gold accents.
- Red ribbon, patterned gift wrap and one or two layered textiles.
- Expressive mantel or shelving vignette with clear focal groups.
- Rich but luminous exposure. Products remain visually distinct from decoration.
- Verified Shopify anchors: noble fir wreath with red bow, red-berry pine spray, tall gold tree and warm lights. Verified Amazon anchors: red plaid runner, pre-lit pinecone garland and gold bell garland. Jewel-tone ornament sets and ribbons are not supported by the current assortment and were not used.

Optional overlay: `More color. More pattern. More joy.`

Pin title: `Maximalist Christmas decor in red, green and gold`

Prompt scaffold:

> Photorealistic editorial maximalist Christmas room in classic red, deep green and warm gold, with abundant greenery, a red bow, berry detail, warm lights and layered festive patterns. Vertical 2:3. Feature destination-specific verified products faithfully and give each a clear visual role. Joyful, luminous and shoppable, not chaotic. No people, brands, logos, fake labels, unsupported text or surreal objects.

## Product-match gate

Before generating each final master or publishing its Shopify page, record:

1. Exact Shopify and Amazon public product URLs.
2. Current availability evidence and check date.
3. Product image references used by image generation.
4. Whether the paired image is byte-identical or a controlled destination-specific variant.
5. Any pictured object that is atmospheric only and not offered for sale.
6. Final Shopify page URL and final Amazon list URL.

Numbered claims are prohibited unless the corresponding public destination count is verified.
