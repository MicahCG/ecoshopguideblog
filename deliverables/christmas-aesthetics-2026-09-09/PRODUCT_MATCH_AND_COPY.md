## Product fidelity

The lifestyle images were created with the built-in image generator from downloaded product-reference images. Prompting prioritized recognizable silhouette, material, color and construction over decorative novelty. The Shopify landing-page grids use real catalog product photos rather than generated approximations.

- White & Gold Shopify: tall gold trees on white marble bases, cream cable-knit stockings and clear glass ornaments. A full-size white tree is ambient styling only and is not presented as sold.
- White & Gold Amazon: ivory cable-knit stockings on gold holders plus the white ceramic village.
- Boho Shopify: Folk Copper plaid throw, natural scalloped stripe napkins and wooden tree lights.
- Boho Amazon: rustic wooden ornaments, orange cheesecloth runner and the rust fleece throw. The Amazon image was regenerated once so the throw matched the verified plush raised-fleece texture and ribbed border rather than a chunky knit.
- Maximalist Shopify: decorated noble fir wreath with red bow, red-berry pine spray, tall gold tree and warm lights.
- Maximalist Amazon: red plaid runner, pre-lit pinecone garland and gold bell garland.

Atmospheric props establish a room, but are not framed as products. Repeated illustrated objects do not imply bundle quantity. Shopify and Amazon products are equivalent by scene role, not identical SKUs. The current Shopify supply supports classic red, green and gold Maximalism, not jewel-tone ornament or ribbon claims.
